Arabic + Egyptian word lists used for exact-hit layout detection.

Modern Standard / general Arabic
- hermitdave/FrequencyWords (OpenSubtitles-derived)
  https://github.com/hermitdave/FrequencyWords
  content/2016/ar/ar_50k.txt (top ~20k filtered)

Egyptian Arabic
- drelhaj/Arabic-Dialects on Hugging Face (CC-BY-4.0)
  https://huggingface.co/datasets/drelhaj/Arabic-Dialects
  EGY frequency list (count >= 3) and EGY-minus-MSA dialect vocabulary
- AdhamAshraf/egyptian-2-arabic on Hugging Face
  https://huggingface.co/datasets/AdhamAshraf/egyptian-2-arabic
  unique tokens from Egyptian sentences (count >= 2)
- arbml/NileULex on Hugging Face (Egyptian/colloquial terms)
  https://huggingface.co/datasets/arbml/NileULex
  single-token Egyptian entries (insult-style ابن/ابو phrases skipped)

English
- first20hours/google-10000-english (Google Trillion Word Corpus)
  https://github.com/first20hours/google-10000-english
  google-10000-english-usa.txt

Arabic personal names
- native/mubaddil/data/arabic_names.txt (أنس خالد محمد صابر and common first names)

Greeting / Egyptian chat seeds are always included.
